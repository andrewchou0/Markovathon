Plan-invalidating event

Any event that makes the plan you are currently executing impossible to execute as written—and obliges someone to decide, quickly, what replaces it.

The event

A rare-earth export licence is suspended indefinitely.

What it invalidates

The build sequence for assemblies that require the magnet billet.

Who decides

A named person, inside hours, with authority to commit to the replacement.
